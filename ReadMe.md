# El Niño Macro-Climate Resilience Advisory

## Overview

The El Niño Macro-Climate Resilience Advisory is a web-based dashboard designed to explore how extreme climate conditions affect agricultural productivity and how tree-canopy interventions may help mitigate heat stress and soil moisture loss.

The current prototype focuses on red gram (pigeonpea / Kandulu), particularly during the flowering and pod-setting stages, under a modeled strong El Niño scenario.

## Key Features

- **Climate scenario analysis:** Compare baseline conditions with different tree-canopy coverage scenarios.
- **Temperature impact assessment:** Examine modeled changes in local temperatures under 0%, 10%, 20%, and 30% additional canopy cover.
- **Soil moisture analysis:** Compare projected soil moisture levels across scenarios.
- **Crop yield risk assessment:** Explore modeled pulse-crop yield losses under varying climate and canopy conditions.
- **Tree survival strategies:** Present drought-management recommendations, including agricultural mulching, subsurface irrigation, shelterbelt clustering, and hydro-absorbent materials.
- **Species recommendations:** Highlight candidate shelterbelt and nurse-tree species such as Kanuga, neem, palmyrah, and subabul.
- **Visual advisory dashboard:** Display comparative metrics, tables, and a biophysical explanation of the modeled relationships.

## Important Note

This repository contains a prototype advisory dashboard. The numerical estimates and scenario outcomes presented in the dashboard should be treated as model assumptions or illustrative outputs until validated against reliable climate observations, crop-yield data, soil measurements, and field experiments. The displayed results do not, by themselves, establish that a particular canopy percentage will fully offset El Niño impacts.

## Future Enhancements

- Integration with satellite-derived vegetation indices such as NDVI

The goal is to support evidence-based exploration of nature-based climate adaptation strategies for agriculture.
